METHOD & ENGINEERING

Built to explain the decision.

Financial model

Monthly amortization with zero-rate handling. Floating debt is immediately recast over its remaining term. Headroom shows how much revenue could fall before the modeled covenant is breached.

Reproducible backend

A Python standard-library engine generates scenario reports and a SQLite database. A SQL view summarizes exposure and coverage by sector.

Verified calculations

Nine Python tests, a published amortization example and 18 cross-language comparisons check that the browser and Python engine agree.

Assumptions and limits

All organizations and financial data are fictional. This is a one-year static stress model: no taxes, capital expenditure, working-capital changes, depreciation adjustments, fees, prepayments, balloon payments, rate caps, collateral valuation or guarantees. DSCR definitions differ across real agreements. Aggregate DSCR does not imply that one borrower can pay another's debt. Covenant breach is not default or expected loss. Nothing here is a lending recommendation.

Formula reference: OTS financial-analysis handbook, hosted by OCC (2009). Used for the mathematical example, not as current regulatory guidance.
